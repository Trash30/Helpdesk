Positioning

Helpdesk VOGO and JIRA: two complementary tools

JIRA is a well-established tool for technical teams. Helpdesk VOGO addresses a different need — operational support — that JIRA isn't designed to cover simply.

💡

Two distinct needs, two complementary tools

JIRA is designed to manage sprints, backlogs and technical issues for a development team. A free tier exists (up to 10 users). It's the right tool for that workflow, and there's no question of replacing it.

VOGO support teams have a different need: managing incoming requests from clubs, partners and leagues, measuring satisfaction, and centralizing customer context. JIRA Service Management could theoretically cover this need, but its free tier is capped at 3 agents and its advanced features are reserved for paid plans — with complexity and cost disproportionate to a non-technical support use case.

Security

Secure by design

A full security audit was carried out across the entire application. 10 issues were identified and fixed. Here are the key measures in place.

🔑

Secure JWT authentication

8-hour tokens, httpOnly cookies (inaccessible to malicious JavaScript), anti-brute-force protection (10 attempts/min).

🛡️

Granular access control

23 permissions configurable per role. Verified server-side on every request — impossible to bypass.

🔒

Secure passwords

Bcrypt hashing, mandatory complexity policy, single-use token reset (24h), never stored in plain text.

📋

Full traceability

Every ticket modification is logged (who, when, old/new value). Immutable audit trail.

🏠

Data on internal network

Application not exposed to the Internet. Data hosted on VOGO's own servers. No third party has access.

✅

Validation on every input

Zod schemas on every endpoint. Protection against injections, XSS, and path traversal.
